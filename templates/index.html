Weather / Intelligence

Semantic search over live National Weather Service alerts and forecast narratives, vectorized into Lakebase.

Reading pipeline status…

Step 1

Sync weather data

Pulls active alerts and multi-day forecast narratives for each location and stores them in weather_documents.

Step 2

Generate embeddings

Chunks every narrative that has no current vector, embeds it, and writes the result to weather_embeddings. Unchanged text is skipped.

Step 3

Search by meaning

Describe the conditions you care about in your own words. Matching is by meaning, not keywords, so wording that never appears in a forecast still finds the right one.
